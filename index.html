HOW THE EXPERIMENT WORKS

Committed before
the answer is known.

A forecast matters here because the schedule is fixed before the auction prices are known. If you can wait for those prices, this simplified problem can be solved directly.

  1. Verified market data70,080 native-quarter prices from Energinet. Negative values retained; gaps and duplicates rejected.
  2. Delayed historical inputs72–336-hour lags and known calendar information. A price is assumed usable 24 hours after its interval ends.
  3. Separate fitting and calibration120 fit days, then 28 calibration days. Select families on June; lock selections for the July–September replay.
  4. Honest constraintsHistorical data may have revisions. This benchmark was used in earlier research. No actual tariffs, plant constraints or order execution are modeled.
